第一部分：全球互联网基石¶

一、 域名注册与管理（Domain Registrars）¶

  1. GoDaddy

    • 官网：www.godaddy.com
    • 主营业务：域名注册、虚拟主机、企业邮箱、网站建设。
    • 覆盖范围：全球。
    • 服务人群：中小企业、个人开发者。
    • 详细介绍：GoDaddy是全球最大的ICANN认证域名注册商，管理着超过8400万个域名。其核心优势在于极低的首年准入门槛和极其丰富的域名后缀选择。作为互联网基建的“门脸”，GoDaddy不仅提供单纯的注册服务，还构建了从域名到主机再到数字营销的一站式生态。其在全球拥有多个数据中心，支撑其庞大的解析请求，其用户界面支持多国语言，是全球站长出海的首选工具之一。

  2. Namecheap

    • 官网：www.namecheap.com
    • 主营业务：域名注册、SSL证书、Whois隐私保护。
    • 覆盖范围：全球。
    • 服务人群：IT专业人士、注重隐私的个人。
    • 详细介绍：Namecheap以“高性价比”和“尊重用户隐私”在业内闻名。与竞争对手不同，它长期提供免费的域名隐私保护服务（WhoisGuard），防止用户信息泄露。其平台操作逻辑简洁，技术支持响应迅速。对于互联网基础设施而言，Namecheap提供了极其稳定的底层注册数据库管理，并集成了自有的DNS服务器，确保了全球范围内的解析速度，是目前最具公信力的二线注册商领头羊。

  3. Dynadot

    • 官网：www.dynadot.com
    • 主营业务：域名拍卖、域名注册、后端API服务。
    • 覆盖范围：北美及亚洲市场。
    • 服务人群：域名投资者、资深互联网用户。
    • 详细介绍：Dynadot是一家技术驱动型的注册商，其简洁的控制面板和透明的定价策略深受好评。它在互联网基建中扮演着重要的“资源流转”角色，其域名拍卖系统是全球二手域名交易的重要场所。Dynadot提供强大的API支持，允许开发者将域名管理功能集成到自己的应用中。其服务器稳定性极高，对于需要长期维护品牌域名的企业来说，是一个极其靠谱的基础设施提供方。

  4. Google Domains (现由Squarespace托管)

    • 官网：domains.google.com
    • 主营业务：域名注册、DNS集成。
    • 覆盖范围：全球。
    • 服务人群：Google Workspace用户、开发者。
    • 详细介绍：原属于Google旗下的基建服务，现虽业务剥离，但其基础设施底座依然强大。它利用Google遍布全球的边缘节点，为用户提供极其快速的DNS响应。其最大的特点是与Google生态的无缝衔接，对于依赖Google Cloud或Workspace的企业，通过其进行域名管理可以获得最佳的路由解析性能。它是互联网基础架构中极简主义的代表。

  5. 阿里云域名 (AliCloud Domains)

    • 官网：www.aliyun.com
    • 主营业务：域名注册、万网空间、国内备案服务。
    • 覆盖范围：中国为主，辐射全球。
    • 服务人群：国内企业、合规性要求高的业务。
    • 详细介绍：收购了曾经的“中国万网”后，阿里云已成为中国最大的域名服务商。它是中国互联网基建的“守门人”，提供了从注册、实名认证、备案到解析的全链路合规服务。其底层设施与阿里云CDN、云盾等安全产品深度打通，对于在国内运营的互联网业务，阿里云提供了最稳固的行政合规与技术支持保障。

  6. 腾讯云域名 (Tencent Cloud Domains)

    • 官网：cloud.tencent.com
    • 主营业务：域名注册、企业微信联动。
    • 覆盖范围：亚太及中国。
    • 服务人群：腾讯生态开发者、游戏及社交应用。
    • 详细介绍：腾讯云在域名领域发力极快，其依托DNSPod（国内顶级解析商）的技术底层，提供了极速的解析体验。腾讯云域名注册服务不仅在价格上有竞争力，更重要的是它与腾讯社交生态的深度集成，比如一键配置企业邮箱、微文档关联等。它是中国互联网基建双雄之一，特别是在抗DDoS攻击和域名安全防护方面，具有业界领先的基建实力。

  7. Name.com

    • 官网：www.name.com
    • 主营业务：国际化域名注册。
    • 覆盖范围：北美、欧洲。
    • 服务人群：小型工作室、欧美站长。
    • 详细介绍：作为早期的域名巨头之一，Name.com在北美市场拥有深厚的基础设施积累。它提供的DNSSEC安全协议支持非常完善，能有效防止域名欺骗和缓存污染。其基建风格偏向稳定和技术化，通过与各种第三方云服务（如Wix, DigitalOcean）的深度整合，成为了连接用户与后端计算资源的桥梁。

  8. Gandi.net

    • 官网：www.gandi.net
    • 主营业务：域名、开源项目托管。
    • 覆盖范围：欧洲及全球。
    • 服务人群：开源社区、极客。
    • 详细介绍：Gandi奉行“No Bullshit”准则，是全球开源社区最信任的基建商之一。它支持数百种后缀，并且在域名安全审计方面极为严格。其基础设施位于欧洲，对于重视数据合规（如GDPR）的互联网企业来说，Gandi是首选的底层供应商。它不仅卖域名，还深度参与互联网基础协议的讨论与制定。

  9. Network Solutions

    • 官网：www.networksolutions.com
    • 主营业务：传统域名注册、企业标识。
    • 覆盖范围：全球。
    • 服务人群：大型传统企业、历史悠久的机构。
    • 详细介绍：它是全球第一家域名注册商，在互联网基建史上具有奠基意义。虽然在移动互联网时代稍显沉重，但其针对大企业的管理服务和专利保护依然顶尖。它的基础设施非常稳健，适合那些对品牌域名安全有极致要求的巨头公司。

  10. Porkbun

    • 官网：porkbun.com
    • 主营业务：廉价域名、SSL。
    • 覆盖范围：全球。
    • 服务人群：个人开发者、创意从业者。
    • 详细介绍：近年来异军突起的基建黑马，以透明的价格和现代化的UI著称。虽然名字俏皮，但其底层API非常强大，支持广泛的自动化操作。对于需要批量部署基础设施的开发者，Porkbun提供了极其友好的接入方式。

二、 权威DNS解析服务（DNS Providers）¶

  1. Cloudflare (DNS)

    • 官网：www.cloudflare.com
    • 主营业务：全球CDN、1.1.1.1公共解析、 authoritative DNS。
    • 覆盖范围：全球300+城市。
    • 服务人群：从个人博客到财富500强。
    • 详细介绍：Cloudflare是现代互联网基建的代名词。其DNS解析速度常年位居全球第一。它不仅提供域名解析，还通过其庞大的Anycast网络，将解析节点部署在离用户最近的地方。它的基建能力在于能瞬间处理TB级的DDoS攻击，其“无限制流量清洗”彻底改变了互联网安全基建的游戏规则。

  2. DNSPod (腾讯云旗下)

    • 官网：www.dnspod.cn
    • 主营业务：智能DNS解析、监控。
    • 覆盖范围：中国及亚太。
    • 服务人群：国内高并发业务。
    • 详细介绍：作为国内最早的智能DNS厂商，DNSPod开创了分线路解析（按电信、联通、移动分流）的先河。在基建层面，它能够根据用户地理位置和运营商实时调度最优IP，极大降低了中国复杂网络环境下的访问延迟。它是国内流量最大、稳定性最强的权威DNS供应商之一。

  3. Akamai (Edge DNS)

    • 官网：www.akamai.com
    • 主营业务：企业级DNS、全球分发。
    • 覆盖范围：全球顶级运营商。
    • 服务人群：政府、银行、顶级电商。
    • 详细介绍：Akamai是CDN的发明者，其Edge DNS服务是目前全球最坚固的解析系统之一。它不面向普通个人用户，而是为承载全球30%互联网流量的巨头提供保障。其基建逻辑是极度的分布式，几乎在每个主流ISP机房都有节点，确保了在极端网络战或大断网情况下的业务连续性。

  4. NS1 (隶属IBM)

    • 官网：ns1.com
    • 主营业务：下一代流量分发、受控解析。
    • 覆盖范围：全球。
    • 服务人群：大型SaaS、流媒体平台。
    • 详细介绍：NS1以“可编程DNS”著称。它的基建允许用户通过API实时改变流量流向，例如根据服务器负载或带宽成本动态切换节点。对于像Dropbox这样需要精准流量调度的公司，NS1是不可或缺的底层逻辑控制中心。

  5. Constellix

    • 官网：constellix.com
    • 主营业务：实时流量调度、DNS监控。
    • 覆盖范围：北美及欧洲。
    • 服务人群：高可用性架构师。
    • 详细介绍：Constellix主打实时监测与响应。它在互联网基建中的作用像是一个精密的流量红绿灯。它能感知全球链路的细微抖动，并自动切换DNS记录。对于金融交易等对延迟极其敏感的行业，它的基建提供了极高的毫秒级决策能力。

  6. Oracle Cloud DNS (原Dyn)

    • 官网：www.oracle.com/cloud/networking/dns/
    • 主营业务：企业级权威解析。
    • 覆盖范围：全球。
    • 服务人群：Oracle生态用户、传统大型IT架构。
    • 详细介绍：Dyn曾是全球最重要的DNS服务商（曾遭遇著名的2016年大断网），后被Oracle收购。即便如此，其基建网络依然是全球骨干网的重要组成部分，支持极其复杂的Geo-location解析，是传统企业云化过程中的重要网络支柱。

  7. Amazon Route 53

    • 官网：aws.amazon.com/route53/
    • 主营业务：云原生DNS、健康检查。
    • 覆盖范围：AWS全球区域。
    • 服务人群：AWS全家桶用户、全栈开发者。
    • 详细介绍：Route 53以“100%可用性SLA”承诺而闻名。作为AWS生态的咽喉，它与ELB负载均衡、S3存储桶深度融合。其基建通过多级冗余确保了在任何单一区域故障时，流量都能迅速重定向，是现代公有云架构中最稳固的基石。

  8. Azure DNS

    • 官网：azure.microsoft.com
    • 主营业务：微软云生态解析。
    • 覆盖范围：全球。
    • 服务人群：Windows生态、跨国集团。
    • 详细介绍：依托微软强大的全球光纤骨干网，Azure DNS提供了极高的响应速度。它在基建上与Active Directory深度打通，是企业内网与公网统一管理的理想选择。其安全性依托于微软每年的巨额安全研发投入，极其可靠。

  9. Vercel DNS (边缘路由)

    • 官网：vercel.com
    • 主营业务：前端部署解析。
    • 覆盖范围：全球边缘节点。
    • 服务人群：Next.js开发者、前端工程师。
    • 详细介绍：Vercel代表了新一代的“无服务器基建”。它不仅提供DNS，更将解析与边缘计算结合。当你修改代码并提交时，Vercel的基建会自动在全球范围内更新路由，让用户永远访问到最近的静态资源节点。

  10. Quad9

    • 官网：www.quad9.net
    • 主营业务：公益性安全DNS。
    • 覆盖范围：全球。
    • 服务人群：注重安全的普通公众及组织。
    • 详细介绍：Quad9（9.9.9.9）是互联网公益基建的代表。由IBM、PCH等联合发起，旨在从DNS层面屏蔽恶意网站和钓鱼链接。其基建节点遍布全球，虽然是免费服务，但其稳定性和安全性足以支撑专业级应用。

三、 CDN与边缘计算（CDN & Edge Services）¶

  1. Fastly

    • 官网：www.fastly.com
    • 主营业务：可编程CDN、实时清除。
    • 覆盖范围：全球高速网络。
    • 服务人群：TikTok、纽约时报等高频更新媒体。
    • 详细介绍：Fastly重新定义了CDN的基建：它允许开发者在边缘端运行代码（VCL）。它的基建特点是“快”，不仅是访问快，更是缓存刷新快（毫秒级）。对于新闻、社交媒体等需要即时分发内容的互联网业务，Fastly是不可替代的底层加速器。

  2. Bunny.net

    • 官网：bunny.net
    • 主营业务：极致性价比CDN、存储。
    • 覆盖范围：全球（特别是非洲、拉美等新兴市场）。
    • 服务人群：独立开发者、视频站长。
    • 详细介绍：Bunny.net是近年来基建领域的破坏者。它以极低的价格提供媲美巨头的性能。其基建网络布局非常精巧，不仅覆盖欧美，在偏远地区的节点质量也非常高。它是中小型企业在预算有限下实现全球加速的最佳选择。

  3. Edgio (由Limelight与EdgeCast合并)

    • 官网：edg.io
    • 主营业务：流媒体加速、Web安全。
    • 覆盖范围：全球大型运营商中转。
    • 服务人群：OTT视频平台、大型游戏下载。
    • 详细介绍：Edgio集成了两家老牌CDN巨头的基建资产。其核心优势在于承载超大规模的并发下载和流媒体直播，是许多3A大游、世界杯直播背后的无名英雄。其基建容量极大，是全球互联网流量消减的主力。

  4. 百度云加速 (Baidu Cloud Acceleration)

    • 官网：su.baidu.com
    • 主营业务：国内CDN、WAF、SEO优化。
    • 覆盖范围：中国境内及部分海外节点。
    • 服务人群：国内中小网站、站长。
    • 详细介绍：百度在基建层面利用了其强大的搜索蜘蛛网络。它的CDN不仅能加速，还能帮助网站更快被百度搜索收录。其在国内拥有海量边缘节点，对于提升国内搜索引擎排名和应对国内网络攻击有天然优势。

  5. KeyCDN

    • 官网：www.keycdn.com
    • 主营业务：透明化CDN服务。
    • 覆盖范围：全球。
    • 服务人群：WordPress用户、中型企业。
    • 详细介绍：KeyCDN以透明、按需付费闻名。其基建架构完全基于SSD存储，确保了静态资源的高I/O响应。它不设最低消费，是开发者在构建起步项目时最常使用的基建服务之一。

  6. CacheFly

    • 官网：www.cachefly.com
    • 主营业务：高吞吐量CDN。
    • 覆盖范围：全球。
    • 服务人群：游戏发行商、大型软件公司。
    • 详细介绍：CacheFly是TCP加速领域的先驱。它的基建专注于大文件的极速分发。在多次全球性的基建测速中，其吞吐量表现非常抢眼。它在边缘节点部署了特殊的传输算法，显著降低了长距离传输的丢包影响。

  7. StackPath (已整合多项服务)

    • 官网：www.stackpath.com
    • 主营业务：边缘计算、容器分发。
    • 覆盖范围：全球主要城市。
    • 服务人群：需要边缘端计算能力的开发者。
    • 详细介绍：StackPath将CDN与计算深度融合。它的基建不仅是分发图片，还能在离用户最近的节点直接运行虚拟机或容器。它是“边缘侧互联网”基建的有力推动者。

  8. CDNetworks

    • 官网：www.cdnetworks.com
    • 主营业务：跨国加速（特别是进出中国）。
    • 覆盖范围：全球，强于亚洲。
    • 服务人群：跨国制造企业、出海品牌。
    • 详细介绍：作为亚洲最大的CDN服务商之一，其基建对于连接东方与西方网络具有独特优势。它在俄罗斯、东南亚等复杂网络环境下拥有极强的穿透能力，是企业全球化部署中的重要网络跳板。

  9. BelugaCDN

    • 官网：www.belugacdn.com
    • 主营业务：轻量化加速。
    • 覆盖范围：全球。
    • 服务人群：初创企业。
    • 详细介绍：利用IPv6等先进基建技术，提供极简的加速方案。虽然规模不及Cloudflare，但其网络节点的链路优化非常纯净，无过度冗余。

  10. CDN77

    • 官网：www.cdn77.com

  1. DE-CIX

    • 官网：www.de-cix.net
    • 主营业务：互联网对等互联交换服务。
    • 覆盖范围：总部法兰克福，节点遍布全球。
    • 服务人群：ISP、云商、内容服务商（Google, Netflix等）。
    • 详细介绍：DE-CIX法兰克福是全球峰值流量最大的互联网交换中心。IXP是互联网的“立交桥”，不同网络在这里直接连接而无需经过昂贵的付费中转。DE-CIX的基建包括了极高性能的核心交换机集群。通过在DE-CIX互联，成千上万个AS号（自治系统）可以实现本地化的流量交换，极大提升了整个欧洲乃至全球互联网的运行效率。

  2. AMS-IX (Amsterdam Internet Exchange)

    • 官网：www.ams-ix.net
    • 主营业务：非营利性流量交换。
    • 覆盖范围：阿姆斯特丹，辐射全球。
    • 服务人群：所有接入互联网的自治系统。
    • 详细介绍：阿姆斯特丹是全球互联网的物理中心之一，而AMS-IX则是其心脏。作为一个非营利组织，它推动了互联网开放对等（Peering）的精神。其基建的可靠性极高，能够处理每秒数万Gb的并发流量。它是欧洲数字经济的基石，确保了欧洲各国之间的数据传输成本几乎为零。

  3. LINX (London Internet Exchange)

    • 官网：www.linx.net
    • 主营业务：流量对等交换。
    • 覆盖范围：英国伦敦及曼彻斯特等。
    • 服务人群：英国及欧洲的互联网从业者。
    • 详细介绍：LINX是全球历史最悠久的IXP之一。它通过分布在伦敦多个机房的分布式架构，确保了即使某个物理地点发生故障，英国的互联网也不会中断。LINX不仅是技术的交换，也是行业政策的制定地，对维护互联网的开放性和中立性起到了关键作用。

  4. Equinix IX

    • 官网：www.equinix.com/interconnection-services/ix/
    • 主营业务：商业化互联网交换。
    • 覆盖范围：全球40多个城市。
    • 服务人群：租用Equinix数据中心的客户。
    • 详细介绍：与DE-CIX等公益性IXP不同，Equinix IX是目前全球最大的商业化互联平台。由于Equinix本身就是全球最大的IDC商，其IX服务具有天然的地理便利性。用户只需在同一大楼内拉一根光纤（Cross-connect），就能与世界上最主要的网络直接打通。它是全球公有云和内容商进行全球分发的首选互联点。

  5. HKIX (Hong Kong Internet eXchange)

    • 官网：www.hkix.net
    • 主营业务：亚洲流量交换枢纽。
    • 覆盖范围：中国香港。
    • 服务人群：亚太地区运营商及内容提供商。
    • 详细介绍：由香港中文大学运营，HKIX是亚洲地区最重要的交换中心。它不仅连接了中国内地三大运营商，还聚集了东南亚、韩国、日本的大量流量。它是中国大陆网络访问海外资源的重要中转站，其基建的稳定性直接影响到整个亚太地区的互联网速度。

Global Marine ensures the survivability of the Internet in sudden global disasters through its maintenance ships and ROVs (underwater robots) distributed in ports around the world.

13. IoT Infrastructure and Gateway (IoT Infrastructure)¶

  1. Advantech (Advantech)

    • Official website：www.advantech.com
    • Main business: Industrial gateway, embedded edge computing, WISE-PaaS platform.
    • Coverage: Global.
    • Serve people: Intelligent manufacturing, transportation, smart city.
    • Detailed introduction: Advantech is synonymous with IoT hardware infrastructure. The various industrial computers and edge gateways it provides are the "translators" that connect physical equipment in the factory to the Internet. Its infrastructure logic is "edge-cloud collaboration", which performs preliminary processing at the first site where data is generated, greatly reducing the pressure on upstream bandwidth. It is the underlying foundation for Industry 4.0 and the Internet to sink into real industries.

  2. Digi International

    • Official website：www.digi.com
    • Main business: Industrial wireless routers, SOM modules, console servers.
    • Coverage: Global.
    • Serve people: Automation engineer, distributed asset management.
    • Detailed introduction: Digi specializes in extremely stable, industrial-grade wireless connections. Its products are often deployed at remote wind farms or in vending machines. As the "glue" of Internet infrastructure, it connects remote terminals that do not have traditional network conditions.

  3. Semtech (LoRa)

    • Official website：www.semtech.com
    • Main business: LoRa chip design, long-distance low-power communication base.
    • Coverage: Global LoRaWAN ecology.
    • Serve people: Internet of Things developers, smart city builders.
    • Detailed introduction: Semtech is the owner of LoRa technology, the global benchmark for Low Power Wide Area Networks (LPWAN). The physical layer protocol it defines allows a sensor to operate for ten years and cover a ten-kilometer radius using only batteries. It is a chip manufacturer that has contributed to the Internet moving towards the era of "Internet of Everything", and has built a low-frequency infrastructure system that is independent of WiFi and cellular networks.

  4. Quectel (Quectel)

    • Official website：www.quectel.com
    • Main business: 5G/LTE/Cat-M wireless communication module.
    • Coverage: Global.
    • Serve people: Internet of Vehicles, POS machines, mobile medical care.
    • Detailed introduction: Quectel is the largest cellular module manufacturer in the world in terms of shipments. It is the "heart" of all physical devices connected to the Internet. Whether it is a shared bicycle or a smart car, most of them run Quectel's modules inside. Its infrastructure contribution lies in encapsulating complex mobile communication protocols into standardized industrial components, which greatly accelerates the popularization of Internet equipment.

  5. Actility

    • Official website：www.actility.com
    • Main business: ThingPark IoT management platform and core network.
    • Coverage: Global Industrial Internet of Things.
    • Serve people: Smart industry, large-scale logistics.
    • Detailed introduction: Actility provides the "operating system" and core network layer of the Internet of Things. It helps enterprise-level customers manage thousands of LoRaWAN or NB-IoT base stations and terminals, and is the control plane in the Internet of Things infrastructure.

14. Network observation, BGP optimization and link measurement (Observability & Traffic Engineering）¶

  1. SolarWinds

    • Official website：www.solarwinds.com
    • Main business: Full stack IT performance monitoring, Orion platform.
    • Coverage: Global.
    • Serve people: Network management, system operation and maintenance, government IT.
    • Detailed introduction: SolarWinds is a “stethoscope” that monitors the health of Internet infrastructure. It can display network topology in real time and monitor the traffic jitter of each router. Although it has encountered major security supply chain incidents, its observation capabilities in complex intranets and large-scale network architectures remain industry standards. It makes the operation of the Internet visual and quantifiable.

  2. Paessler (PRTG)

    • Official website：www.paessler.com
    • Main business: All-round network monitoring.
    • Coverage: Global.
    • Serve people: IT teams of medium and large enterprises.
    • Detailed introduction: PRTG is famous for “everything can be monitored”. Its infrastructure monitoring covers all dimensions from traffic protocol analysis to temperature and humidity sensing. To maintain the stability of the Internet Data Center (IDC), PRTG provides an extremely refined real-time warning mechanism.

  3. Kentik

    • Official website：www.kentik.com
    • Main business: Network observability, cloud network diagnostics.
    • Coverage: Global cloud and hybrid architecture.
    • Serve people: Network architect, CDN engineer.
    • Detailed introduction:Kentik is the darling of modern high-performance network operation and maintenance. It can process massive flow data (Flow Data) and help engineers find out why data packets from New York to Shanghai are lost. The value of its infrastructure lies in its ability to observe congestion on Internet paths from a global perspective and provide optimization solutions.

  4. ThousandEyes (part of Cisco)

    • Official website：www.thousandeyes.com
    • Main business: End-to-end path observation, Internet map.
    • Coverage: Thousands of nodes around the world.
    • Serve people: SaaS companies (such as Salesforce), major cloud customers.
    • Detailed introduction: ThousandEyes has constructed an "Internet weather forecast map" by deploying a large number of detection points around the world. When a cross-border optical cable breaks or a DNS service goes down, it is usually the first system to call the fire alarm. It is the digital guarantee for the high availability of modern Internet services.

  5. Noction

    • Official website：www.noction.com
    • Main business: IRP (Intelligent Routing Platform), BGP automated optimization.
    • Coverage: Global backbone network exchange point.
    • Serve people: Large-scale ISPs and game manufacturers with multi-exit BGP networks.
    • Detailed introduction: The BGP protocol is naturally based on the "shortest path" rather than the "fastest path". Noction’s infrastructure logic is to break this mediocrity. It detects the link quality of different operators in real time and automatically modifies BGP policies so that traffic always takes the smoothest path. It is the ultimate optimizer of Internet transmission efficiency.

  6. Zabbix

    • Official website：www.zabbix.com
    • Main business: Open source enterprise-grade monitoring.
    • Coverage: Global open source community.
    • Serve people: IT managers who value autonomy and control.
    • Detailed introduction: As the world's most successful open source infrastructure monitoring software, Zabbix has supported the initial construction of countless small and medium-sized Internet companies. Its extremely high flexibility allows developers to customize any monitoring logic, and it is one of the most democratic and broadest infrastructures in the field of Internet infrastructure monitoring.

  7. Catchpoint

    • Official website：www.catchpoint.com
    • Main business: User experience monitoring (DEM), edge observation.
    • Coverage: Global.
    • Serve people: Businesses that are extremely sensitive to delays (such as ad bidding).
    • Detailed introduction: Catchpoint measures the Internet from a user's perspective. Its detection nodes are not only in the computer room, but also in real home broadband and mobile networks. The real link data it provides is an important basis for Internet companies to make global distribution node (PoP) layout decisions.

  8. Splunk (part of Cisco)

    • Official website：www.splunk.com
    • Main business: Machine data analysis, log auditing, and security operations.
    • Coverage: Global large and medium-sized enterprises.
    • Serve people: Security Director, Data Analyst.
    • Detailed introduction: Splunk is the “black box” of Internet infrastructure. It can devour massive logs from firewalls, routers, and servers and dig out the root causes of failures. Its infrastructure value lies in transforming disordered underlying data into actionable security decisions.

  9. Nagios

    • Official website：www.nagios.org
    • Main business: Basic monitoring of IT infrastructure.
    • Coverage: Global.
    • Serve people: Veteran operation and maintenance expert, traditional computer room management.
    • Detailed introduction: Nagios is the originator of the monitoring world. Its architecture is extremely rigorous and its support for various underlying protocols is unparalleled. Although the UI is traditional, its authority in ensuring the underlying survival status of servers and network hardware is unquestionable.

  10. Grafana Labs

    • Official website：grafana.com
    • Main business: Data visualization dashboard, Prometheus integration.
    • Coverage: Global cloud native ecosystem.
    • Serve people: DevOps engineer, full-stack developer.
    • Detailed introduction: Grafana is the "dashboard" of modern Internet infrastructure. It turns boring low-level performance metrics into cool, easy-to-understand charts. By cooperating with infrastructure components such as Prometheus and Loki, it defines the monitoring aesthetics and standards in the cloud native era.

15. Web3 and Decentralized Internet Infrastructure (Decentralized Infrastructure)¶

  1. Alchemy

    • Official website：www.alchemy.com
    • Main business: Blockchain node infrastructure, API platform.
    • Coverage: Global Blockchain Network.
    • Serve people: Web3 developers, chain game companies, NFT platforms.
    • Detailed introduction: Alchemy is called the “AWS of the blockchain world”. Building Web3 applications requires connecting to blockchain nodes, but maintaining nodes is extremely expensive and unstable. Alchemy provides a high-performance, highly available fully managed node API, allowing developers to develop decentralized applications without having to manage the underlying hardware. Its infrastructure supports the operation of giants such as OpenSea and is an efficient bridge connecting the traditional Internet and blockchain networks.

  2. Infura (part of ConsenSys)

    • Official website：www.infura.io
    • Main business: Ethereum/IPFS node access.
    • Coverage: Global.
    • Serve people: Ethereum ecosystem developer, MetaMask user.
    • Detailed introduction: Infura is the pioneer of Web3 infrastructure. The data requests behind the MetaMask wallet used by most users are actually sent to Infura's computer room. It is the throat of the Ethereum ecosystem. Although its centralized operation method is controversial in the community, its actual traffic and stability make it an indispensable underlying base in the Web3 world.

  3. QuickNode

    • Official website：www.quicknode.com
    • Main business: Multi-chain high-performance node service.
    • Coverage: More than 10 public chain networks around the world.
    • Serve people: Web3 project pursuing extreme speed.
    • Detailed introduction: QuickNode is known for its speed. Through its globally distributed accelerator network, it enables on-chain queries to be several times faster than standard nodes. At the Internet infrastructure level, it proves that even on top of a decentralized protocol, a commercial-grade experience can still be achieved through excellent network layer optimization.

  4. Protocol Labs (IPFS/Filecoin)

    • Official website：protocol.ai
    • Main business: Decentralized storage protocol, peer-to-peer network base.
    • Coverage: global node.
    • Serve people: An organization that focuses on sustainable storage of content.
    • Detailed introduction: IPFS aims to replace the HTTP protocol. It does not find content by IP address, but by the "hash value" of the content. This infrastructure model completely changes the storage and distribution logic of data. Its associated project Filecoin provides an incentivized storage market and is the physical basis for building an “Internet that will never disappear”.

  5. The Graph

    • Official website：thegraph.com
    • Main business: Decentralized indexing protocol.
    • Coverage: Global.
    • Serve people: All Web3 applications that need to query data on the chain.
    • Detailed introduction: If the blockchain is a database, then The Graph is the Google of Web3. It establishes a fast query API for messy on-chain data through distributed indexer nodes. It is an extremely critical "data middle layer" in Web3 infrastructure.

  6. Arweave

    • Official website：www.arweave.org
    • Main business: Permanent decentralized storage.
    • Coverage: Global.
    • Serve people: Historical archive storage and censorship-resistant content release.
    • Detailed introduction: Arweave proposed the concept of paying once and storing for a hundred years. Its infrastructure adopts a structure similar to a "woven network" and uses unique algorithms to ensure that data is continuously copied and saved. It is the physical cornerstone of the Internet's "digital library."

  7. Chainlink

    • Official website：chain.link
    • Main business: Decentralized Oracle Network (Oracle).
    • Coverage: Global.
    • Serve people: DeFi, smart contracts, insurance, games.
    • Detailed introduction: Blockchain is a closed system and cannot know external information (such as oil prices, weather). Chainlink securely and reliably feeds real-world data into the chain through its vast network of validators. It is the "trusted gateway" between Web3 infrastructure and traditional real-world data.

  8. Pocket Network (POKT)

    • Official website：www.pokt.network
    • Main business: Decentralized RPC node market.
    • Coverage: Tens of thousands of independent nodes around the world.
    • Serve people: A project party pursuing ultimate decentralization.
    • Detailed introduction: Unlike Alchemy, Pocket uses a DAO model to allow tens of thousands of independent computer owners to provide node services. Its infrastructure eliminates dependence on a single cloud vendor and is the ultimate solution for building an "attack-resistant and censorship-resistant" Internet architecture.

  9. Circle (USDC)

    • Official website：www.circle.com
    • Main business:Digital currency financial infrastructure and payment rail.
    • Coverage: Global Internet financial ecology.
    • Serve people: Internet finance, multinational traders.
    • Detailed introduction: USDC issued by Circle is the "anchor" of the Internet's native value exchange. It has reshaped the clearing and settlement infrastructure through blockchain technology, making global fund flows as instant and low-cost as sending emails. It is the “compliance settlement layer” that connects the modern Internet and the global financial system.

  10. Ankr

    • Official website：www.ankr.com
    • Main business: Cross-chain node hosting and Staking infrastructure.
    • Coverage: Global.
    • Serve people: Node operators, ordinary investors.
    • Detailed introduction: Ankr uses idle IDC computing power to provide cheap blockchain node services. It is a model of "secondary utilization of resources" in Internet infrastructure, providing extremely low-threshold hardware support for the rapid popularization of Web3.

16. Special Infrastructure and Future Pillars (Supplementary)¶

  1. Cloudflare (Workers & R2) - As introduced in the first part, its object storage infrastructure is supplemented here.

    • Detailed introduction: Cloudflare’s R2 storage does not charge egress traffic fees and is challenging Amazon S3’s infrastructure hegemony. Its Workers edge computing allows Internet applications to completely bid farewell to the concept of "server" and is the pinnacle of Serverless infrastructure.

  2. HashiCorp (Terraform)

    • Official website：www.hashicorp.com
    • Main business: Infrastructure as code (IaC).
    • Detailed introduction: Without Terraform, building the facilities for the 150 companies mentioned above would be extremely slow. It defines a language for managing hardware with code and is the "construction manual" for the automation construction of modern Internet infrastructure.

  3. Docker / Mirantis

    • Official website：www.docker.com
    • Main business: Containerization standard.
    • Detailed introduction: Docker packages software into standard "containers". It allows Internet applications to ignore differences in underlying hardware and run in any IDC. It is the biggest contributor to the improvement of infrastructure efficiency in the past decade.

  4. Red Hat (OpenShift)

    • Official website：www.redhat.com
    • Main business: Enterprise-grade Linux and Kubernetes.
    • Detailed introduction: As the guardian of open source infrastructure, Red Hat ensures the security and compliance of the underlying operating system of the Internet. OpenShift is currently the world’s most stable hybrid cloud container base.

  5. Figma (Design Infrastructure)

    • Official website：www.figma.com
    • Detailed introduction: The “blueprint” of Internet products is here. As a collaboration tool, it is not only software, but also the infrastructure for collaboration and creative processes in the entire Internet industry.

  6. Stripe (payment infrastructure)

    • Official website：www.stripe.com
    • Detailed introduction: The “checkout counter” of the Internet. Stripe solves the world's cumbersome tax and payment interfaces with a few lines of code, and is the digital blood on which the commercial Internet depends.

  7. Twillio (communication API)

    • Official website：www.twilio.com
    • Detailed introduction: Turn the traditional telecommunications network into a line of APIs. Behind every SMS verification code on the Internet, Twillio's global communication relay station may be running.

  8. OpenAI (computing infrastructure)

    • Official website：openai.com
    • Detailed introduction: Through a huge GPU cluster, OpenAI is defining "AI as Infrastructure". In the future, Internet traffic may no longer be generated by people, but by its reasoning infrastructure.

  9. NVIDIA (DGX Cloud)

    • Official website：www.nvidia.com
    • Detailed introduction: NVIDIA not only provides chips, but also directly provides cloud computing computer rooms. It is the ultimate promoter of the Internet's shift from pure connectivity to computing.

  10. ICANN (Internet Corporation for Assigned Names and Numbers)

    • Official website：www.icann.org
    • Main business: Global domain name system, IP address management.
    • Detailed introduction: It is the "ultimate orchestrator" of all Internet infrastructure. Although it is a non-profit organization, it manages the root servers and assigns all AS numbers and IP addresses. Without ICANN, these 150 companies would become islands. It is the highest consensus point of human Internet civilization.

The above is a list of 150 Internet infrastructure-related institutions and companies organized into 15 subdivisions. Industry mergers and acquisitions and business adjustments are changing rapidly, and we will update from time to time if we see changes in the future.
